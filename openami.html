OpenAMI

Open-Source Advanced Metering Infrastructure for Rural Energy Access

What is OpenAMI?

OpenAMI brings actionable energy telemetry to village mini-grids — per-customer reads, credit balance, tamper alerts, and remote disconnect. Not just aggregate data, but leakage visibility at every layer (feeder → branch → customer) so operators can locate theft and loss, not just see imbalance.

Why it matters now: Proprietary vendor lock-in left operators stranded when platforms shut down. OpenAMI provides a vendor-agnostic path: open gateways, MQTT northbound interop, geo asset topology, and operator backends you can self-host or migrate between providers.

The Problem

Metering capex rivals generation costs. Proprietary stacks lock operators in. When vendors exit, meters become bricks. Rural backhaul is fragile. Protocol fragmentation (STS, DLMS, Modbus, OEM mesh) blocks swap and scale.

OpenAMI Solution

Vendor-agnostic gateways at the pole. MQTT/JSON northbound contract. DLMS/COSEM at the meter. Open backends (MPM, ThunderCloud 2.0, OpenEMS). Geo topology for loss mapping. Community-maintained integration.

⚠ Architecture Reality Check

OpenAMI is not an any-to-any solution matrix. You must pick compatible components: First-km hardware (meter/DCU) → Aggregation hub (SparkNet / MeshEMS / MQTT gateway) → Backend (MPM / OpenEMS / ThunderCloud) → Billing & O&M. Integrations are evolving; not every backend supports every meter type yet.
