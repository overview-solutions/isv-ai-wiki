Online pre-session (already ran)

Tuesday 22 September 2026, 11:00–12:30 CEST. Hosted by AMDA, A2EI, and EnAccess so more voices than the Nairobi room could be heard. Pre-read says 50+ attendees on that call. Themes recorded in the outcomes pack (no chat names copied here):

  • Escrow of meter keys — optional fallback (A2EI named as escrow in the EarthSpark developer note), not a mandate that operators surrender keys.
  • EarthSpark encryption keys — keys unlock comms including firmware update via SparkNet-HTTP (perpetual, royalty-free license to a lawful SparkMeter owner, per the outcomes note).
  • Scope beyond two vendors — Calin and other OEMs, DLMS-wide support, Okra mesh, STS certification cost, multi-HES complexity, Madagascar-style tax rules, weak OEM field support.
  • Self-host burden — operators who take hardware/software/cloud still need a shared open customer-management codebase rather than each rebuilding a full platform.

EarthSpark key options (pre-read PDF)

Developer note in the pack, after ThunderCloud 2.0 / SparkNet-HTTP open-source: three paths — (1) EarthSpark keeps holding keys, (2) copy into A2EI escrow, (3) request keys (or a chosen custodian) from EarthSpark. Options 2 and 3 can run together. Process URLs stay on EarthSpark’s own channels — not duplicated here.
