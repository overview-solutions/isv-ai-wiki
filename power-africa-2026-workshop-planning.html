Hands-on Street-EMS / MeshEMS for in-country engineers on village mini-grids. Compare commercial AMI (SteamaCo, SparkMeter) with open OpenAMI cabinets. Who: utility and distribution engineers, minigrid operators, metering people, founders, researchers, energy-access practitioners.
